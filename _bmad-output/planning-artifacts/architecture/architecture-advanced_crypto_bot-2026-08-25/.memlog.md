- (constraint) [ADOPTED] MVP covers all spot-IDR pairs that pass point-in-time dynamic eligibility; it is not restricted to a static most-liquid-pairs list.
- (constraint) [ADOPTED] Primary trading horizon is swing intraday through multi-day; every decision is namespaced by Horizon ID.
- (constraint) [ADOPTED] Maximum notional for one Position is 10 percent of decision-time canonical equity; total open exposure is 40 percent; daily-loss latch is 2 percent; hard portfolio drawdown trigger is 10 percent.
- (constraint) [ADOPTED] Hard drawdown is a risk-reduction trigger toward zero exposure, not a guaranteed realized-loss cap; stale data or venue failure never permits fabricated valuation.
- (constraint) [ADOPTED] Legacy strategy may become Champion only after canonical conformance and all evidence gates; incumbent status gives no privilege.
- (decision) AD-01: Use a hexagonal modular monolith. Domain and application layers depend only inward; venue, storage, transport, dashboard, Telegram, and legacy compatibility are adapters.
- (decision) AD-02: Namespace canonical aggregates by portfolio_id, experiment_id, strategy_version, horizon_id, instrument_id, and writer_epoch; use UTC event and receive time; use fixed-point integer or Decimal for money and quantity, never SQLite REAL.
- (decision) AD-03: One immutable CandidateSnapshot yields exactly one CanonicalDecision per policy version. Gates may annotate or veto but no downstream component may overwrite action, reason, boundary, or snapshot reference.
- (decision) AD-04: Action legality is state-dependent: ABSTAIN only without a Position, HOLD only with a Position, ENTER increases exposure, EXIT carries explicit target quantity. Protective precedence is operator kill, hard drawdown, hard invalidation or stop, reconciliation, profit or trailing or time stop, alpha EXIT, HOLD.
- (decision) AD-05: Append-only lifecycle facts are canonical. Only Fill changes cash and exposure; the Fill journal is the sole accounting authority. Legacy trades, pending orders, balances, positions, Redis, and UI tables are rebuildable compatibility projections.
- (decision) AD-06: One CommandPort owns mutations and commits aggregate state, canonical event, projection checkpoint, and transactional outbox atomically. Consumers are idempotent and cannot mutate trading state.
- (decision) AD-07: Exactly one active writer is enforced by a persistent fenced writer_epoch. Local process locks are insufficient; stale epochs are rejected at every mutation boundary.
- (decision) AD-08: Every decision consumes one immutable point-in-time MarketSnapshot containing event and receive time, source offsets, freshness, book and trade data, instrument rules, and universe eligibility. Later refetch cannot alter that decision.
- (decision) AD-09: Champion and Challengers implement pure StrategyPort contracts and receive the same CandidateSnapshot and cost assumptions. Their ledgers and policy state are isolated; only approved Champion output can reach intent creation.
- (decision) AD-10: Net edge after fees, taxes, spread, slippage, latency, and uncertainty must clear a preregistered boundary or the policy chooses ABSTAIN or HOLD. Calibration and change alarms can block ENTER and reduce risk but cannot create alpha or suppress protective EXIT.
- (decision) AD-11: A persisted RiskGovernor computes all limits from one canonical equity snapshot, applies fixed precedence, and owns latched kill, daily-loss, drawdown, stale-data, unknown-order, and reconciliation states. Sizing inputs can only reduce exposure from the hard envelope.
- (decision) AD-12: VenuePort and deterministic simulator share one order lifecycle. Only OrderCoordinator may submit or cancel. Deterministic client_order_id and idempotency keys prevent duplicate effect; timeout becomes UNKNOWN until venue recovery proves the state.
- (decision) AD-13: Indodax market and private streams are sequence-aware. Gaps, disconnects, or ambiguous acknowledgements trigger REST recovery and reconciliation before new exposure. A websocket message is evidence, not automatically canonical fact.
- (decision) AD-14: Deterministic replay freezes market snapshot, universe, portfolio and risk starting state, venue cursors, fee and instrument rules, code and image digest, dependency lock, schema, strategy, model, calibrator, cost model, config, seed, and clock inputs. Replay suppresses all external side effects.
- (decision) AD-15: Every experiment has a preregistered Evidence Specification and append-only trial ledger. Promotion reads a sealed report with leakage-safe windows, costed outcomes, calibration, effective sample size, tail and drawdown, concentration, DSR, PBO, and benchmark comparison; promotion is never automatic.
- (decision) AD-16: SQLite canonical storage uses one application writer and short transactions. Redis is non-authoritative cache or reliable transport only when durability policy is explicit; cache eviction and transport queues must be separate.
- (decision) AD-17: Dashboard and Telegram read versioned projections asynchronously. Write actions use a separate authenticated audited command surface; notification or dashboard failure cannot stall canonical trading commits.
- (decision) AD-18: Migration uses a strangler at ports but account-wide trading authority has a hard cutover. Legacy and replacement may shadow-read simultaneously, but they never both settle or submit orders. High-water mark, fencing generation, reconciliation, and rollback are additive and auditable.
- (decision) AD-19: Deployment is reproducible from a locked dependency graph and immutable image digest with SBOM. The embedded SQLite runtime must be at least 3.51.3 or an official fixed backport; older affected WAL runtimes fail startup.
- (decision) AD-20: DRY RUN replacement processes physically exclude live private credentials and live submission adapters. Enabling real trading is a separate post-MVP security and approval change, not a configuration toggle inside this release.
- (decision) AD-21: Safety invariants are absolute gates; operational availability and latency are SLOs; trading profitability and calibration are evidence KPIs. Degraded mode blocks ENTER first while preserving reconciliation and protective EXIT where venue evidence permits.
